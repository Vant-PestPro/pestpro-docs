Program Overview

This General Health Pest Control Service Agreement establishes the terms under which Pest Pro, LLC will provide ongoing pest management services to the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando, a skilled nursing and rehabilitation facility managed by Excelsior Care Group. All services described in this agreement are designed to support the facility's Integrated Pest Management program and are provided in accordance with the standards applicable to healthcare environments, including skilled nursing facilities regulated under Florida Administrative Code Chapter 59A-4 and subject to inspection by the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS).

Services will commence on Monday, March 23, 2026, and will be performed on the first four Mondays of each calendar month unless otherwise agreed upon in writing by both parties.

Service Areas

Services provided under this agreement cover the full interior and exterior of the facility, including all of the following areas:

  • 80 resident rooms — 40 rooms on the East Wing and 40 rooms on the West Wing
  • Dietary kitchen, associated food storage areas, and kitchen offices
  • Dining room and service station
  • Activities room
  • Courtyard patio
  • Front lobby and reception areas
  • Administrative offices
  • Storage rooms and mechanical and utility rooms
  • Two nurses stations with adjacent nourishment rooms (one per wing)
  • Restrooms, hallways, and all common areas throughout the facility
  • Full exterior perimeter of the building

Pricing and Payment Terms

The monthly service rate for all services described in this agreement is $800.00 per month, plus any applicable Orange County, Florida sales tax. This rate is guaranteed for a period of one year from the effective date of this agreement. Following the initial one-year term, the monthly service rate is subject to an annual increase not to exceed 3% per year, with written notice provided no less than 30 days prior to any rate adjustment.

Invoices will be issued at the end of each service month and are due and payable within 30 days of the invoice date (Net 30). Invoices will be delivered to the Regional Maintenance Director and Chief Engineer, as well as any additional contacts designated by the facility in writing. Emergency service visits are billed separately at $180.00 per visit upon completion of service.

On-Site Documentation and Recordkeeping

Pest Pro, LLC will provide and maintain a physical service log binder to be kept on-site at the location designated by facility management. This log will be available at all times for review by facility staff, management, and regulatory inspectors. In addition to the on-site binder, Pest Pro utilizes internal CRM software to generate work orders and maintain digital service records.

Copies of current pest control business licenses and certificates of insurance will be provided to the facility along with this agreement and updated upon renewal.

IPM and Healthcare Facility Standards

All pest control services provided under this agreement will be performed in accordance with Integrated Pest Management (IPM) principles as recommended for licensed healthcare environments. This includes the prioritization of inspection and monitoring, source identification, non-chemical corrective measures, and targeted pesticide applications only when necessary and appropriate.

All pesticide products used will be EPA-registered and applied strictly in accordance with label directions and applicable federal and state regulations. Within patient-occupied areas, nursing stations, and food service environments, applications will be limited to crack and crevice treatments, enclosed bait stations, and mechanical control devices. Broad or general surface applications will not be made in occupied or sensitive areas of the facility.

Regulatory Scope Limitation. Pest Pro, LLC provides pest control services as described in this agreement. The responsibility for compliance with all applicable AHCA, CMS, and state and federal healthcare facility regulations, including the maintenance of required documentation and the implementation of facility-wide sanitation and environmental standards, remains solely with the facility operator and management. Pest Pro's services are designed to support the facility's pest management and IPM program but do not constitute a guarantee of regulatory compliance or survey readiness.

Client Responsibilities

The effectiveness of this pest management program is directly dependent upon the cooperation and diligence of the facility's management and environmental services staff. The following responsibilities are required of the client throughout the duration of this agreement.

  • Maintain sanitary conditions throughout all service areas, including prompt cleanup of food spills, moisture removal, and elimination of harborage conditions.
  • Ensure all trash receptacles within the dietary kitchen and food preparation areas are emptied and discarded nightly. No food, even if covered, shall be left out in the dietary kitchen or food preparation and storage areas after hours.
  • Maintain all structural elements, including sealing of gaps, cracks, and penetrations that may serve as pest entry points.
  • Ensure all trees, overhanging limbs, shrubs, and vegetation are trimmed and maintained away from the building structure.
  • Keep all dumpster and refuse areas clear of accumulated debris and discarded food matter.
  • Designate a single point of contact for routine scheduling and communication, and a clear escalation contact for urgent or emergency situations.
  • Ensure appropriate access is provided to all scheduled service areas on service days, including any areas requiring escorted entry.
  • Report any observed pest activity to Pest Pro promptly via the facility's designated communication channel.
  • Maintain the on-site service log binder in the designated location and ensure it remains accessible at all times.

Pest Pro Response Commitments

  • Routine service performed on the first four Mondays of each calendar month.
  • All reported pest activity will be addressed on the next scheduled service visit.
  • Emergency service calls responded to within 24 hours, usually the same day.
  • All calls and text messages returned within 4 hours during normal business hours.
  • Written service reports provided after each visit and maintained in the on-site binder.
