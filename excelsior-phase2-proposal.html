Overview

Pest Pro, LLC submits this proposal to expand our existing pest management agreement with Excelsior Care Group to seven additional skilled nursing and rehabilitation facilities. We currently provide monthly GHP services to the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando under an active contract. This proposal extends that same program to the remaining properties in the Excelsior portfolio.

Each facility will receive a comprehensive, all-inclusive Integrated Pest Management program tailored to the requirements of a licensed healthcare setting. Dietary kitchen service, rodent bait station monitoring, and emergency callbacks for covered pests are included in the flat monthly rate at every property. There are no add-on charges for these services.

Pest Pro currently serves the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando under a monthly GHP agreement, providing weekly service, full-facility coverage, and AHCA-compliant documentation. This proposal applies that same contract structure to seven additional Excelsior Care Group facilities.

Service Schedule

Each facility will be serviced on a scheduled basis, with frequency determined by the current pest activity status of the property. During the initial service period, while activity is being assessed and brought under control, each facility will receive four visits per month. Once pest activity has been eliminated and the property is confirmed stable, service transitions to two visits per month on a routine maintenance schedule.

If activity is identified or reported at any point during the maintenance schedule, service frequency returns to four visits per month at the same flat monthly rate. There is no additional charge for increased service frequency. All service days are established in advance by mutual agreement and may be adjusted with advance coordination.

All service schedules are held to. Pest Pro does not reduce visits or defer service without prior notice and facility approval. Digital service reports are delivered to designated contacts after every visit.

What Is and Is Not Included

The scope below applies uniformly to all facilities covered by this proposal and aligns with the terms of the existing RCO monthly service agreement:

✓ Included at Flat Monthly Rate

  • Cockroaches, all species including German roach
  • Palmetto bugs and American roaches
  • General ants (see exclusions)
  • Spiders
  • Silverfish and firebrats
  • Rodents, rats and mice, interior and exterior
  • House flies and drain flies
  • Stored product pests
  • Occasional invaders: crickets, earwigs, centipedes

✗ Not Included · Quoted Separately

  • Fleas and ticks

  • Wildlife, snakes, and nuisance animals
  • Termites (Pest Pro is not licensed for termite control)
  • Stinging or venomous insects

Healthcare Compliance and IPM Standards

All services are performed in accordance with Integrated Pest Management principles as required for licensed healthcare facilities under Florida Administrative Code Chapter 59A-4, and as applicable to AHCA and CMS inspection standards.

Every product applied is EPA-registered and selected for compatibility with a 24-hour occupied care environment. Applications are limited to crack and crevice, enclosed bait systems, and targeted treatments. No broadcast or open-space applications are made in patient care areas. All technicians assigned to Excelsior Care Group facilities are trained and experienced in healthcare pest management environments.

Service documentation is formatted to support survey preparation. Each report includes the date, technician name, areas inspected and treated, products applied, and pest activity observed. Copies of the current FDACS pest control business license and certificates of insurance are included with this proposal and updated annually.
