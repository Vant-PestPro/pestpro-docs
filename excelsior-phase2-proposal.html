Service Frequency Program

All Phase II facilities will be enrolled in a two-phase service frequency program designed to establish control, eliminate confirmed activity, and transition to an efficient long-term maintenance schedule:

4× per Month (Weekly)
Applied during the initial service period while pest activity is being identified, treated, and eliminated. Frequency continues at 4×/month until the property is confirmed under control. No additional charge above the flat monthly rate.
2× per Month (Bi-Monthly)
Recurring service schedule once the property is stabilized and confirmed pest-free. Same flat monthly rate — no change in pricing upon transition to maintenance. If activity resurges at any time, frequency increases back to 4×/month at no additional charge.
No additional charges for increased frequency. If pest activity is identified or reported at any point during the maintenance phase, service frequency will immediately return to 4×/month until the issue is resolved — at the same flat monthly rate. This is included as standard, not billed as an extra service.

IPM Standards and Healthcare Compliance

All pest management services are performed in strict accordance with Integrated Pest Management (IPM) principles, as recommended by the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS) and applicable guidelines for licensed healthcare facilities subject to AHCA and CMS oversight.

Our approach prioritizes:

  • Inspection and monitoring first — identifying the source, extent, and contributing conditions before any product application
  • Exclusion and sanitation guidance — actionable recommendations to eliminate conditions conducive to pest entry and harborage
  • Targeted, low-impact applications — EPA-registered products at the minimum effective rate, with full preference for enclosed bait systems and crack-and-crevice methods in all sensitive and occupied areas
  • Documentation and traceability — digital service reports delivered after every visit, suitable for AHCA and CMS inspection review
Healthcare Survey Readiness. Our service documentation is designed to support each facility's survey preparation. Digital reports include service date, areas treated, products used, pest activity observed, and technician information. Copies of current FDACS pest control business license and certificates of insurance are provided with this proposal and updated annually upon renewal.
