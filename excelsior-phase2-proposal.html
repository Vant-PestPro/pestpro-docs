Overview

Pest Pro, LLC submits this proposal to expand its existing pest management relationship with Excelsior Care Group to seven additional skilled nursing and rehabilitation facilities within the portfolio. This proposal covers the properties listed herein and reflects a multi-property discounted rate extended in recognition of the full portfolio relationship.

This document is a service proposal. If accepted, a separate service agreement may be executed for each individual property, as required. Any facility may be excluded from the proposed arrangement without effect on the remaining properties.

Each property in this proposal presents its own conditions and pest pressures. Service at every location will be built around a custom-tailored Integrated Pest Management program designed for the specific needs, layout, and regulatory requirements of that facility.

Pest elimination cannot be guaranteed where structural deficiencies, unresolved repairs, or sanitation conditions remain unaddressed. Pest Pro will identify and document these conditions at each visit and communicate findings directly to the appropriate facility contacts. Remediation of structural and sanitation issues is the responsibility of the facility.

Service Frequency

Service at each facility begins at four visits per month. This schedule remains in effect until pest activity is eliminated and the property is confirmed stable with no active pest pressure. At that point, service transitions to two visits per month and remains at that frequency.

Should pest activity recur at any time during the reduced-frequency schedule, service returns to four visits per month and continues at that rate until the property is once again pest free and confirmed stable. The monthly rate does not change at any point in this cycle. There is no additional charge for increased service frequency, and no separate authorization is required for Pest Pro to respond.

Service Coverage

Every service visit covers the complete interior and exterior of the facility. All resident wings and common areas are worked on a rotating documented schedule so that every area of the property receives inspection and treatment within each service cycle. Dietary kitchen and all rodent bait stations are serviced on every cycle without exception.

Service areas include: all resident rooms and wings, dietary kitchen and food storage areas, dining rooms and nourishment stations, nursing stations, all common areas and hallways, activity and therapy rooms, administrative and staff offices, storage and mechanical rooms, laundry facilities, and the full exterior perimeter.

Treatment Approach

All products are EPA-registered and selected for use in occupied healthcare environments. Applications are crack and crevice, enclosed bait stations, and targeted treatments only. No broadcast or open-space applications are made in patient care areas. In the dietary kitchen, all products meet food-service compliance standards.

Service logbooks are maintained at each property and updated following each visit. Monthly summary reports are provided to designated contacts and are available at any time upon request. Reports are formatted to support AHCA and CMS survey documentation needs. All Pest Pro technicians are state-licensed and trained for service in occupied healthcare environments. A Certificate of Insurance is available upon request. Product safety data sheets are available upon request for all materials in use at any property.

Our Commitments

  • Pest Pro will not cancel or defer any scheduled service visit without advance notice to the facility. If a visit cannot be completed as scheduled, it will be rescheduled within the same service cycle
  • A dedicated technician familiar with each facility's layout and operations is assigned to that property
  • Emergency callbacks for covered pests are responded to within 24 hours, typically the same day, at no additional charge
  • All calls and messages are returned within four hours during normal service hours
  • Dietary kitchen and rodent monitoring are completed on every service cycle without exception
  • A certified pest control operator is reachable by phone during normal service hours for any concern that cannot be resolved at the technician level

Facility Responsibilities

  • Sanitary conditions maintained in all service areas, including prompt cleanup of food spills and moisture sources
  • Dietary kitchen trash emptied and secured nightly; no uncovered food left in kitchen or storage after hours
  • Structural gaps, cracks, and utility penetrations reported to Pest Pro when identified
  • Vegetation and landscaping kept trimmed away from the building perimeter
  • Dumpster and refuse areas kept clean and free of accumulated debris
  • Access provided to all areas on scheduled service days
  • Pest activity reported promptly to the assigned technician or our office
  • One point of contact designated per facility for scheduling, with a secondary contact for urgent matters
  • The primary responsibility for notifying residents, employees, and individuals with health sensitivities regarding pest control services rests with facility administration. Pest Pro will provide advance notice of scheduled service to the designated facility contact. It is the facility's responsibility to inform any individuals who may require precautionary measures.

Regulatory Scope

Pest Pro, LLC provides pest control services as described in this proposal. The responsibility for compliance with all applicable AHCA, CMS, and state and federal healthcare facility regulations, including the maintenance of required documentation and the implementation of facility-wide sanitation and environmental standards, rests with the facility operator and management. Pest Pro's program is designed to support each facility's pest management objectives but does not constitute a guarantee of regulatory compliance or survey readiness.
