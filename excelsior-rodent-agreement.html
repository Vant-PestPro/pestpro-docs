Program Overview

Pest Pro, LLC is pleased to report that the first week of the Rodent Eradication Program at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando has been completed with significant results. Progress during the initial service week has exceeded our expectations and we are currently ahead of our projected timeline. We are confident that this positive trajectory will continue through the duration of the program.

This agreement outlines the full scope of the Rodent Eradication Program, including the week-by-week service schedule, pricing, responsibilities of all parties, and the terms and conditions governing the services provided by Pest Pro, LLC.

Target Areas of Service

The following areas have been identified as primary and secondary zones of concern and will be addressed throughout the duration of this program.

Interior Target Areas: Dietary kitchen and all associated storage areas, office areas, and common areas throughout the facility.

Exterior Target Areas: Full building perimeter, roof penetrations including lead stacks, and all points where air conditioning equipment is secured to the structure.

Commercial grade deodorizer will be applied to affected areas as needed throughout the course of this program.

Client and Maintenance Staff Responsibilities

The results of this program are directly dependent upon the cooperation and prompt action of the client's property management and maintenance team. The following responsibilities must be fulfilled to ensure the effectiveness of treatment and to achieve the projected timeline for full control.

  • Complete all exclusion work and structural repairs to seal interior and exterior rodent entry points.
  • Ensure all trees, overhanging limbs, shrubs, and bushes are trimmed and maintained away from the building structure.
  • Remove and maintain all excess leaves and debris from all areas surrounding the building.
  • Maintain proper sanitation in all areas, including pepper decking, ceiling tiles, and drop ceiling spaces.
  • Report any observed pest activity to the designated site log or directly to the Pest Pro account manager without delay.
  • Ensure all doors and other points of entry are maintained and kept closed to deter additional rodent entry.
  • Keep all dumpster areas, trash areas, and surrounding entrances clear of discarded food and unsanitary debris.

Pest Pro Response Commitments

  • Any reported pest activity will be addressed within 24 hours of notice.
  • All calls and text messages will be returned within 4 hours.
  • A written weekly progress report will be provided at the conclusion of each service week, addressing observations, actions taken, and progress to date.

Pests Covered

This service agreement is specific to rodent eradication (rats and mice) at the above-referenced property. This agreement does not cover termites, bed bugs, fleas, poisonous or stinging insects, spiders, Pharaoh ants, carpenter ants, acrobat ants, snakes, or wildlife including raccoons, possums, or squirrels, unless otherwise specified in writing.
