Week 3 Executive Summary

Week 3 of the Rodent Eradication Program at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando continues the trend of measurable, sustained progress. The active rodent population within the facility has declined significantly from the levels observed during the program's initial weeks, and the coordinated efforts of both Pest Pro service personnel and the Excelsior maintenance team are producing the results this program was designed to deliver.

Week 3 Status: Continued Decline — Program Progressing as Expected. Pest Pro service personnel located and removed a total of 2 rodents from the dietary areas during Week 3 service visits — a substantial reduction from the capture volumes of Weeks 1 and 2, reflecting the effectiveness of the ongoing treatment and exclusion strategy. Evidence of at least one additional rodent casualty was identified within the drop ceiling and wall void spaces; however, this individual could not be fully located or retrieved due to access limitations. On the structural remediation front, maintenance staff have completed the sealing of roof penetration points, as documented in the photographic record accompanying this report. Final-stage work, including the repair and sealing of HVAC ducting and perimeter tree trimming, is currently scheduled or already underway.

Week 3 Activities and Observations

Services were conducted at a three-times-per-week frequency during Week 3, consistent with the service adjustment communicated in the Week 2 report. The following activities and conditions were observed and addressed during this reporting period.

  • Rodent Removal — Dietary Areas: Pest Pro service personnel located and removed 2 rodents from the dietary areas during Week 3 service visits. All mechanical traps, glue boards, and monitoring stations were checked, reset, and rebaited at each visit in accordance with program protocols.
  • Additional Casualty — Void Spaces: Evidence of at least one additional rodent casualty was detected within the drop ceiling and/or wall void spaces. This individual could not be fully located or physically retrieved due to the nature of the access limitations within those structural areas. The presence of this casualty is consistent with program effectiveness and is not cause for concern; odor dissipation is expected within a normal timeframe.
  • Roof Penetration Points — Sealed by Maintenance: Maintenance staff have completed the sealing of identified roof penetration points during Week 3. Photographic documentation of this work has been provided and is included in this report. The closure of these access points represents a critical milestone in the facility's long-term exclusion efforts and is commended.
  • HVAC Ducting — Repair and Sealing: Remediation of the damaged HVAC ducting above the drop ceiling in the dietary area — one of the most significant structural vulnerabilities identified at the program's outset — is either currently scheduled or already underway. Completion of this work remains a high-priority item and must be brought to full resolution before the program can be considered structurally complete.
  • Tree Trimming: Perimeter tree trimming, which addresses the exterior harborage and potential roof access conditions contributing to ongoing rodent pressure on the building, is currently scheduled or already underway. This item is directly linked to the program's long-term success and should be completed without delay.
  • Interior and Perimeter Conditions: Interior conditions within the dietary areas and adjacent spaces continue to show improvement. Pest Pro service personnel will continue to monitor all treatment zones at each remaining service visit and will report any change in activity levels immediately.

Looking Ahead — Week 4

Week 4 service visits are currently underway on a three-times-per-week schedule beginning April 3, 2026. Pest Pro personnel will continue to focus on the dietary kitchen and all associated areas, above-ceiling void spaces, mechanical rooms, and the building perimeter. All monitoring devices will be checked, serviced, and rebaited at each visit.

At the conclusion of Week 4, Pest Pro will conduct a formal end-of-program assessment to evaluate the current state of the infestation, the completeness of structural remediation, and the appropriate path forward. A Week 4 progress report will be issued at that time and will include a recommendation regarding continued service, transition to general pest maintenance, or both.

The measurable decline in active rodent population and the ongoing completion of critical structural work are strong indicators that this program is achieving its intended outcome. Pest Pro remains fully committed to this facility and to delivering a complete and lasting resolution. We commend the maintenance team and staff at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando for their continued cooperation and diligence throughout this process.
