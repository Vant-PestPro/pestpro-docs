Week 4 Executive Summary

Week 4 of the Rodent Eradication Program at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando marks the successful conclusion of the active eradication phase. The program has achieved its primary objective: full elimination of the active rodent infestation that had persisted at this facility for over two years. The results documented across all four weeks of service reflect the effectiveness of Pest Pro's treatment strategy and, critically, the outstanding cooperation, responsiveness, and commitment of the Excelsior maintenance staff throughout the entire program.

Program Status: Eradication Phase Complete — Objective Achieved. During Week 4 service visits, Pest Pro personnel located and removed one adult rodent from the dietary area drop ceiling. No further rodent activity was reported or observed anywhere in the facility beyond this single recovery. Infrared motion cameras installed in the dietary ceiling have captured no evidence of ongoing rodent movement or activity. Tree trimming has been completed. The program has achieved the level of control required to transition to an elevated monitoring posture integrated within the regular weekly General Pest Health (GHP) service schedule.

Week 4 Activities and Observations

Services were conducted at a three-times-per-week frequency during Week 4, consistent with the service schedule established at the conclusion of Week 2. The following activities and conditions were observed and addressed during this final reporting period.

  • Rodent Removal — Dietary Drop Ceiling: One adult rodent was located and removed from the drop ceiling in the dietary area during Week 4 service visits. All mechanical traps, glue boards, and monitoring stations were checked, reset, and rebaited at each visit in accordance with program protocols. This single recovery represents a continued and dramatic decline from the capture volumes documented in earlier weeks of the program.
  • No Further Reported Activity: Beyond the single rodent located and removed by Pest Pro service personnel, no additional rodent activity was reported or noted anywhere within the facility during the Week 4 service period. No sightings, no audible signs of movement, and no evidence of new activity were identified by staff, maintenance personnel, or Pest Pro service technicians during any visit.
  • Infrared Motion Camera Monitoring — Dietary Ceiling: Infrared motion-activated cameras installed within the dietary ceiling area have been actively monitoring for movement throughout the service period. As of the close of Week 4, the cameras have not captured any evidence of ongoing rodent activity in the dietary ceiling space. This independent verification of cessation confirms the effectiveness of the eradication program and provides objective documentation for the facility's compliance and AHCA records.
  • HVAC Duct Repair — Actively Underway: Repair and remediation of the damaged HVAC ducting above the dietary drop ceiling is actively underway. Completion of this work remains essential to the long-term structural integrity of the exclusion effort. Pest Pro strongly encourages the maintenance team to bring this item to full resolution at the earliest opportunity.
  • Damaged Ceiling Tiles — Replacement in Progress: Maintenance staff are actively removing and replacing damaged ceiling tiles in the affected areas. This remediation work is commended and directly supports the program's goal of eliminating residual harborage conditions and returning the facility to full operational condition.
  • Tree Trimming — Completed: Perimeter tree trimming has been completed. This item was one of the key exterior conditions identified during the initial program assessment as a contributing factor to sustained exterior rodent pressure on the building. Its completion is a meaningful and commended step in reducing the long-term reinfestation risk for this facility.
  • Bait Station Maintenance: All existing rodent bait stations (RBS) on the property were inspected, maintained, and serviced during Week 4 visits. Ongoing monitoring and maintenance of these stations will be incorporated into the regular weekly GHP service schedule going forward.

Looking Ahead — Transition to Ongoing Monitoring

The Rodent Eradication Program at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando has been successfully executed. The active infestation that had persisted at this facility for over two years has been eliminated through a coordinated four-week eradication strategy combining mechanical trapping, targeted exclusion, structural remediation, and ongoing monitoring.

Pest Pro commends the entire Excelsior Care Group team — maintenance staff, dietary staff, and facility management — for their outstanding cooperation, responsiveness, and commitment throughout this program. The level of staff engagement and the speed at which structural remediation items were addressed were directly instrumental in achieving these results within the program timeline.

Going forward, Pest Pro's transition plan for this property is as follows:

  • Weeks 5–6 (April 10–23, 2026): Elevated monitoring integrated into the regular weekly GHP service visits. Pest Pro personnel will inspect all monitoring stations, bait stations, and previously active areas at each visit and report any notable activity or additional findings immediately. No additional cost to the facility.
  • Ongoing (Beyond Week 6): If no further rodent activity is identified through the close of Week 6, Pest Pro will continue to closely monitor the property for any signs of rodent activity as part of the regular ongoing GHP service schedule. All existing rodent bait stations (RBS) on the property will be regularly inspected and maintained at each service visit, providing a permanent early-warning system against future infestation.
  • Reporting: Any notable findings during the Weeks 5–6 monitoring period will be communicated to Thomas and George promptly. If no activity is identified, a brief final status confirmation will be issued at the close of Week 6.
Remaining Priority Items for Maintenance: Pest Pro recommends that the facility prioritize the completion of HVAC duct repair and ceiling tile replacement as soon as possible. These structural items, while not currently presenting an active infestation risk, represent the last remaining vulnerabilities and should be resolved to fully protect the long-term results of this program.
