Executive Summary

This report covers Weeks 5 and 6 of the Rodent Eradication Program at the Rehabilitation Center of Orlando, representing the final monitoring phase of the program. The monitoring period concluded on April 24, 2026.

Program Status: Complete. All Clear. Throughout the entirety of the Weeks 5 and 6 monitoring period, there were no reports, no sightings, and no evidence of any rodent activity anywhere within the facility. This outcome is consistent with the trajectory established during the active eradication phase and confirms that the program has achieved its objective. The Rodent Eradication Program is now formally concluded, and ongoing vigilance will be maintained as an integrated component of the regular scheduled General Pest Health (GHP) service.

Weeks 5 & 6 Monitoring Activities and Findings

As outlined in the Week 4 Final Report, Weeks 5 and 6 transitioned from active eradication to an elevated monitoring posture, fully integrated within the regular weekly General Pest Health (GHP) service schedule. All previously established monitoring stations and bait stations were inspected and serviced at each visit during this period.

  • No Rodent Activity Detected: Pest Pro service personnel conducted thorough inspections of all previously active and high-risk areas throughout the facility during Weeks 5 and 6. At no point during this monitoring period was any evidence of rodent activity identified. No sightings, no audible signs of movement, and no physical evidence of activity were reported by facility staff, maintenance personnel, or Pest Pro technicians.
  • All Monitoring Stations Clear: All mechanical traps, glue boards, and bait stations placed throughout the facility were inspected at each service visit. No captures and no signs of activity were recorded at any station during this period.
  • No Activity Reports from Staff or Management: No reports of rodent sightings or concerns were received from dietary staff, maintenance personnel, or facility management during the Weeks 5 and 6 monitoring period.
The results documented during Weeks 5 and 6 represent the strongest confirmation of program success to date. Following a sustained period of zero activity across all areas of the facility, the Rodent Eradication Program has achieved a complete and verified resolution of the infestation that had been present at this property for over two years.
