Overview

Pest Pro, LLC is pleased to submit this proposal to Mestdagh, Wall & Hamilton, P.A. for monthly exterior pest management services at two commercial properties in Longwood and Sanford, Florida. This proposal covers both properties under a single service agreement and reflects combined portfolio pricing.

Following our on-site assessments at both locations, we identified active pest pressures at each property that are well-suited to a structured exterior maintenance program. Our program is built on Integrated Pest Management principles, with each property receiving a service approach tailored to its specific structures, layout, and environmental conditions.

This proposal covers exterior pest management only. Interior pest control services are not included in this agreement. Should individual tenants within either structure require interior service, Pest Pro, LLC is available to work directly with those tenants under a separate service agreement at their discretion and cost.

Site Assessment

Pest Pro conducted a site assessment at both properties prior to preparing this proposal. The following observations informed the scope and pricing of this program.

390 S. Ronald Reagan Blvd — Longwood, FL 32750

This property is an older metal commercial warehouse structure with corrugated metal eaves along the full roofline. During our assessment we observed significant wasp and mud dauber activity throughout the eave system, with active nests built into virtually every corrugated channel along the structure. An existing rodent bait station was also found on-site, indicating a prior or ongoing rodent issue at this location. Mature trees adjacent to and overhanging the structure create additional pest access points and harborage opportunities.

Building exterior and perimeter — Longwood
Active mud dauber nesting in corrugated eave channels
Existing rodent bait station found on-site — confirmed rodent pressure
Active wasp nest at eave corner junction
2551 McCracken Rd — Sanford, FL 32771

This property is a large modern commercial warehouse complex with expansive grounds. Our assessment identified multiple large active fire ant colonies throughout the turf and parking areas, with mounding concentrated along the building foundation and adjacent landscape beds. The property features a retention pond along one boundary and a drainage corridor running the length of the building — both of which are known attractants for rodent activity and represent ongoing pressure points. Wasp nesting activity was also observed along the eave system on the building exterior.

Wasp nesting observed along building eave system

Service Scope

Each monthly visit includes a complete exterior perimeter treatment of all building foundations, entry points, utility penetrations, eaves, soffits, window frames, door frames, and accessible exterior harborage areas at each property. All three components of this program — general pest maintenance, rodent prevention, and wasp/stinging insect control — are performed at every scheduled visit.

Service is performed in accordance with Integrated Pest Management principles using EPA-registered products selected for commercial environments. Applications are targeted and applied to exterior surfaces only. Products are chosen to provide lasting residual protection while minimizing unnecessary chemical exposure for tenants and building occupants.

Service Component Description Frequency
General Pest Maintenance Full exterior perimeter treatment targeting ants, cockroaches, spiders, crickets, beetles, and other general household and commercial pests. Includes foundation, entry points, landscape beds, and harborage areas. Monthly
Rodent Prevention Exterior rodent monitoring and preventative treatment. Inspection of perimeter for active signs of rodent activity; tamper-resistant bait station service where applicable. Both properties present environmental conditions — water features, drainage corridors, and adjacent landscaping — that make ongoing rodent monitoring essential. Monthly
Wasp & Stinging Insect Control Inspection and treatment of all eaves, overhangs, eave joints, corrugated channels, and structural gaps for active wasp, mud dauber, and yellow jacket nesting. Both properties present significant nesting conditions warranting consistent attention. Monthly
