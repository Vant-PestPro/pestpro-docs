What Is GHP? — General Household Pest Coverage

GHP stands for General Household Pest, which is the category of pest control licensed by the State of Florida covering the most common pests found in and around Central Florida homes. Here is a full breakdown of what is included in your GHP exterior protection:

  • Ants (all species)
  • Roaches and Palmetto Bugs (all species)
  • Spiders (including black widows and wolf spiders)
  • Wasps and Hornets (exterior nesting activity)
  • Centipedes and Millipedes
  • Crickets
  • Earwigs
  • Silverfish
  • Sowbugs and Pillbugs
  • Beetles

We treat the perimeter of the structure, entry points, eaves, harborage zones, and landscaped areas where pest activity is most common. The goal is to stop them on the outside before they ever make it in.
