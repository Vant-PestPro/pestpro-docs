Overview

Pest Pro, LLC is pleased to submit this proposal to 5Central Capital for monthly exterior pest management service at Hickory Landing in Lakeland and Lucia Apartments in Winter Haven. This proposal reflects combined portfolio pricing and presents both properties as a single service account.

Our program is built on Integrated Pest Management principles, with each property receiving a service approach tailored to its specific structures, layout, and pest pressures. We do not apply a one-size-fits-all contract. Hickory Landing spans four separate duplex structures across three parcels. Lucia Apartments encompasses multiple residential buildings, including the main multi-unit structure and several duplex configurations. Each building perimeter, foundation, entry point, and harborage area within the improved parcels is worked on every service visit.

Pest Pro focuses on exterior preventative maintenance to establish and maintain a pest-free perimeter around each residential structure. Effective results depend on routine access to all building exteriors and reasonably maintained common areas. Pest Pro will communicate any conditions that may limit treatment effectiveness directly to the designated property contact.

Service Scope

Each monthly visit includes a full exterior perimeter treatment of all residential building foundations, entry points, utility penetrations, eaves, soffits, window frames, door frames, and accessible exterior harborage areas within the improved parcels described in this proposal.

Service is performed in accordance with Integrated Pest Management principles using EPA-registered products selected for residential environments. All applications are targeted and applied to exterior surfaces only under the standard monthly program. Products are chosen to provide lasting residual protection while minimizing unnecessary chemical exposure.
