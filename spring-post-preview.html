🌿 Spring in Central Florida = Pest Season Three pests that spike every April — and what to watch for: 🐜 Fire Ants Warmer soil temps send colonies expanding. Watch for new mounds in your yard, especially after rain. 🦟 Mosquitoes Breeding season starts now. Any standing water, even a bottle cap, is enough. Dump it. 🪳 Cockroaches German roaches thrive in the humidity spike. If you're seeing them during the day, you already have an infestation. Early action beats a full treatment every time.
