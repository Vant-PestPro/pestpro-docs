How Pest Pro Works

Our tools, our team, and how it all fits together

Every tool we use has a specific job. Nothing overlaps, nothing gets dropped. Vant (our AI) connects them all — so the team focuses on the work, not the paperwork.
Our Tools
🗂️
FieldworkHQ
Where the work gets done
  • All scheduled jobs, routes, and service visits
  • Technician dispatching — David and Brandon see their assignments here
  • Client records, service history, and notes
  • Invoicing and payment tracking
  • The single source of truth for what happened in the field
✅
ClickUp
Where the team stays organized
  • Projects, tasks, and to-dos for every person on the team
  • Active client accounts — one folder per client, everything tracked
  • Sales pipeline — leads from first contact to signed contract
  • Admin and billing tasks for Anne
  • SOPs, training materials, and onboarding checklists
  • Internal projects — website, marketing, systems
📣
GoHighLevel
Where new clients come from
  • CRM — every prospect and lead tracked from first touch
  • Automated follow-up sequences (email, text, voicemail)
  • Ad campaign landing pages and lead capture forms
  • Review requests sent automatically after service
  • When a lead becomes a client → they move to FieldworkHQ
📧
Email
Where clients reach us
  • Client emails monitored in real time by Vant
  • Urgent messages flagged to Daniel immediately
  • Service reports and proposals sent from Vant@PestProLLC.com
  • Billing communications handled by Anne from info@PestProLLC.com
🦟
Vant (AI)
The glue that connects everything
  • Monitors email, ClickUp, and FieldworkHQ simultaneously
  • Creates and updates tasks automatically based on real events
  • Builds reports, proposals, and documents on demand
  • Sends Daniel and Anne a daily briefing — done, pending, overdue
  • Flags anything slipping before it becomes a problem
  • Available 24/7 via Telegram
How It Works in Real Life
📩 Scenario 1
Thomas at Excelsior emails about a rodent sighting
  • Vant reads the email within minutes
  • Daniel gets a Telegram alert immediately
  • A task is created in ClickUp → Excelsior Care Group
  • David gets assigned and sees it in his task list
  • Job gets scheduled in FieldworkHQ
📣 Scenario 2
A new lead comes in from a Google ad
  • Lead captured in GoHighLevel automatically
  • Automated follow-up text sent within 5 minutes
  • Vant flags Daniel if it's a commercial account
  • Lead appears in ClickUp Sales pipeline
  • Once signed → moved to FieldworkHQ as active client
🌅 Scenario 3
Monday morning briefing
  • Vant pulls everything from ClickUp + FieldworkHQ
  • Summarizes: what's scheduled, what's overdue, what needs a decision
  • Daniel gets it on Telegram before 9 AM
  • Anne gets the billing version — invoices due, payments pending
