Opening & Prevention

"Bedbugs spread through movement of items — not by themselves."
  • Visual scan before stripping beds — every time, every room
  • Keep linens contained at all times
  • Never place linens or carts on beds
  • No cross-contact between rooms

Code I — How to Inspect Beds

"Focus on the head of the bed. That is where they are most often found."
🔍 Deploy laminated photos now. Pass in controlled order. No interruptions.
Tier 1 — Every Time
  • Mattress seams & piping
  • Top corners of bed skirt
  • Box spring underside
  • Headboard — front, back & mount points
  • Fabric folds & crevices
Tier 2 — Within 6–8 Ft
  • Bed frame joints
  • Nightstands
  • Lamps & alarm clocks
  • Wall outlets
  • Baseboards / carpet edge
Tier 3 — Occasional
  • Curtains (pleats & seams)
🧪 Deploy live samples now. Control passing — do not interrupt training flow.

Linen Handling — Contamination Control

"Sheet → cart → next room = spread."
  • Bag linens inside the room
  • Seal bag before removal
  • Do NOT transport loose linens under any circumstances

Reporting Standard

"Clear reporting prevents delays and prevents spread."
  • Room number
  • Exact location — bed, kitchen, patio, etc.
  • What was observed: live insects · staining · nesting

CLOSING LINE — DELIVER THIS EXACTLY:

"Early detection keeps it in one room.
Improper handling spreads it across the property."

⚠ Failure Points — Watch For These

  • Staff continuing to clean after suspicion
  • Linens moved without bagging first
  • Headboards & bed skirt tops skipped
  • Vague or incomplete reporting

Translator Rules — Non-Negotiable

  • One sentence → full stop → translate → continue
  • No overlapping speech
  • "Stop work immediately"
  • "Do not move contaminated items"
  • "Bag linens inside the room"
  • "Report immediately"
