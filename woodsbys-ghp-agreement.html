Program Overview

This General Health Pest Control Service Agreement establishes the terms under which Pest Pro, LLC will provide ongoing pest management services to Woodsby's Cafe, a licensed food service establishment located at 4515 South Orange Blossom Trail, Kissimmee, FL 34746. All services are performed in accordance with Integrated Pest Management (IPM) standards applicable to commercial food service facilities and in compliance with all applicable Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS) regulations.

Services commenced on March 15, 2026, and were performed after regular business hours in the evening, as agreed with the Client's manager, Victor Moya. David Kell, Pest Pro's Senior Lead Technician, has been designated as the assigned technician for this account and has been provided facility access keys by the Client for the purpose of performing after-hours service visits.

Key Assignment: David Kell, Senior Lead Technician, is the designated service technician for this account. Facility access keys have been provided by Victor Moya, Manager, for after-hours service access.

Scope of Services

Pest Pro, LLC will provide comprehensive general pest management services covering the full interior and exterior of the facility. Services include, but are not limited to:

  • Full interior inspection of all areas including kitchen, food preparation and storage areas, dining room, restrooms, storage rooms, and all common areas.
  • Full exterior perimeter inspection and treatment as necessary.
  • Deployment, monitoring, and servicing of mechanical traps, glue boards, and rodent monitoring stations in applicable areas.
  • Rodent monitoring and control throughout the facility, including exterior perimeter stations where applicable.
  • Targeted pest treatment applied as conditions warrant, using IPM-appropriate methods for a licensed food service environment.
  • All pesticide applications within the kitchen and food preparation areas will be limited to crack and crevice treatments, enclosed bait stations, and mechanical control devices only. No broadcast or general surface applications will be made in food contact or food preparation areas.
  • Written service documentation provided after each service visit.

Pests Covered and Excluded

Included in Monthly Service

  • Ants (general species)
  • Roaches and Palmetto Bugs
  • Spiders
  • Silverfish
  • Rodents (Rats and Mice)
  • General household pest insects (GHP)

Not Included — Additional Charge

  • Fire, Pharaoh, Carpenter, and Argentine Ants
  • Fleas and Ticks
  • Bed Bugs
  • Bees and Wasps
  • Snakes and Wildlife
  • Stinging or Poisonous Insects

Non-included pest services are available upon request and will be quoted and invoiced separately at the time of service.

Client Responsibilities

The effectiveness of this pest management program is directly dependent upon the cooperation and maintenance practices of the Client. The Client agrees to the following for the duration of this Agreement:

  • Maintain proper sanitation in all areas of the facility, including kitchens, food storage, and waste disposal areas.
  • Ensure all trash receptacles in the kitchen and food preparation areas are emptied and secured each night. No food shall be left exposed in food preparation or storage areas after hours.
  • Keep all dumpster areas, exterior trash enclosures, and surrounding building entrances clear of discarded food and unsanitary debris.
  • Ensure all doors, windows, and other potential pest entry points are maintained and kept closed when not in active use.
  • Promptly notify Pest Pro of any observed pest activity between scheduled service visits.
  • Cooperate with recommendations for structural repairs or exclusion work identified as necessary to maintain program effectiveness.
  • Ensure safe and unobstructed access to all service areas at each scheduled visit.

Pest Pro Response Commitments

  • Scheduled service performed monthly after business hours, as mutually agreed.
  • Any reported pest activity will be addressed within 24 hours of notice for covered pests.
  • All calls and messages will be returned within 4 business hours.
  • Written service documentation provided after each scheduled visit.
  • All services performed by licensed, insured, and trained pest control professionals in compliance with Florida law and food service IPM standards.
