Agreement Overview

This Rodent Exclusion Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Pest Pro, LLC ("Service Provider") and Woodsby's Cafe ("Client"), effective as of the Service Start Date listed above. This Agreement authorizes Pest Pro, LLC to perform rodent exclusion and ongoing rodent management services at the above-referenced location in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth herein.

All services will be performed after business hours in the evening as agreed with the Client's designated manager, Victor Moya, to minimize disruption to daily operations.

Key Assignment: David Kell, Pest Pro's Senior Lead Technician, has been designated as the assigned service technician for this account. Mr. Kell has been provided facility access keys by Victor Moya, Manager, for the purpose of performing scheduled after-hours service visits.

Scope of Services

Pest Pro, LLC will provide a comprehensive Rodent Exclusion Program designed to eliminate existing rodent activity, identify and seal points of entry, and implement ongoing monitoring to prevent future infestation. Services are specific to rodents (rats and mice) and include the following:

  • Initial assessment and identification of all active rodent entry points, harborage areas, and travel routes throughout the facility.
  • Deployment and monitoring of mechanical traps, glue boards, and rodent monitoring stations throughout identified active areas.
  • Application of commercial-grade rodenticide in tamper-resistant bait stations in accordance with all applicable Florida Department of Agriculture regulations for food service facilities.
  • Identification and documentation of all structural gaps, penetrations, and entry points recommended for exclusion by the Client's maintenance team.
  • Interior and exterior perimeter inspection at each scheduled service visit.
  • Written service documentation provided after each visit.
  • Ongoing communication and reporting to the designated Client contact.

All services are performed in compliance with applicable Florida pest control regulations and integrated pest management (IPM) standards appropriate for a licensed food service establishment.

Client Responsibilities

The effectiveness of this program depends in part on the Client's cooperation and maintenance practices. The Client agrees to the following throughout the duration of this Agreement:

  • Maintain proper sanitation in all areas of the facility, including food preparation, storage, and waste areas.
  • Ensure all trash receptacles in kitchen and food service areas are emptied and secured each night. No food shall be left exposed in the kitchen or food preparation areas after hours.
  • Keep all dumpster areas and exterior trash enclosures clear of discarded food and unsanitary debris.
  • Ensure all doors, windows, and other potential points of entry are maintained and kept closed when not in active use.
  • Promptly notify Pest Pro of any observed pest activity between scheduled service visits.
  • Cooperate with any recommended exclusion repairs or structural modifications identified by the technician as necessary to maintain program effectiveness.
  • Ensure safe and unobstructed access to all areas of the facility required for service at each scheduled visit.

Pest Pro Response Commitments

  • Any reported pest activity will be addressed within 24 hours of written or verbal notice from the Client.
  • All calls and messages from the Client will be returned within 4 business hours.
  • Written service documentation will be provided following each scheduled service visit.
  • All services will be performed by licensed, insured, and trained pest control professionals in accordance with Florida law and IPM best practices.

Term and 30-Day Exit Clause

This Agreement shall commence on the Service Start Date and continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter. Notice may be delivered by email, certified mail, or hand delivery to the contact information listed in this Agreement.

In the event of termination, the Client remains responsible for payment of all services rendered prior to the effective date of termination. No partial month refunds will be issued. Pest Pro, LLC reserves the right to terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice in the event of non-payment or a material breach of the Client's obligations under this Agreement.

Florida Notice: Pursuant to Florida law, this Agreement includes a mandatory 30-day written notice period prior to termination by either party. This clause applies to all ongoing pest control service agreements entered into within the State of Florida.
